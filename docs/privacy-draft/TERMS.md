## In plain words

Maven helps you plan for a target role: it builds a roadmap of milestones, scores your progress with simple arithmetic, and writes CV bullets from the work you finish. The AI suggestions are drafts. Check every roadmap and CV bullet before you rely on it or put it on a CV. Your data is yours. You can export or delete it at any time.

## 1. Accepting these terms

By using Maven you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app. [[LEGAL: minimum age to accept, and how this applies to users below the age of majority.]]

## 2. What Maven is

Maven is a mobile app that:

- builds a roadmap of milestones for a target role from what you tell it;
- calculates a readiness score from your finished milestones, using arithmetic only (no AI);
- writes a draft CV bullet when you finish a milestone;
- optionally fills in your experience from a CV PDF you choose;
- optionally keeps your data in sync across devices if you sign in with Google.

AI features use Google Gemini through Maven's server and only run if you agree to AI processing (see the Privacy Policy).

## 3. Your account

- You can use Maven without an account.
- If you sign in with Google, you are responsible for keeping access to that Google account. Anyone who can sign in to it can see your synced Maven data.
- **Sign out and clear this device** removes your data from that phone but keeps your account and its synced copy.
- **Delete account** deletes your account and its synced copy (see the Privacy Policy for what remains).

## 4. Your content

- Your experience text, notes, roadmaps and CV bullets ("your content") remain yours.
- You give [[OPERATOR NAME]] permission to process your content only to provide Maven to you: storing it if you use sync, and sending it to the AI provider when you use AI features. [[LEGAL: confirm the scope and wording of this licence.]]
- Only submit content you have the right to share. Remove other people's personal information and anything sensitive before you submit it.
- **AI output is a draft.** Roadmaps, extracted experience and CV bullets can be wrong or incomplete. Maven marks a bullet that needs a real number and never invents one for you, but you are responsible for checking that every claim on your CV is true.
