2. Description of the App
Maven is a personal productivity and progress-tracking application designed to help users break down experiences and targets into actionable checklists, track daily progress, receive local notifications, and generate professional CV bullet points based on completed tasks.

AI Features: The App utilizes third-party artificial intelligence services strictly for generating initial setup items based on your input and for converting completed tasks into CV lines. Once generated, these results are stored locally on your device.
