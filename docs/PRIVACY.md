App Name: Maven

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

At Maven ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy. Because Maven is designed as a local-first application, we prioritize keeping your data on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect
Unlike many apps, Maven does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. We do not collect, store, or have access to your personal identifiers (such as your name, email address, or phone number) on external servers.

2. How Data Is Handled on Your Device
Checklists and Progress: All your checklist items, daily statuses, and progress calculations are stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not sync this data to external cloud servers.

Notifications: Reminders and notifications are managed locally through your device's operating system based on local logic (e.g., tracking items in progress for over 14 days). No notification data is sent to external servers.

3. Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) Services
Maven uses external AI APIs strictly for two functional moments:

Initial Setup: When you input your background and targets, your text is sent to an AI service once to generate your customized checklist.

CV Lines: When you mark an item as complete, the AI formats it into a professional CV line.

Note: Data sent to AI providers is processed transiently to generate the requested output and is not used to build persistent user profiles by us. Once the output is delivered to your device, it is saved locally.

4. Third-Party Services
The App may utilize standard operating system frameworks (such as local notification managers) or third-party AI APIs. These services operate under their respective privacy policies. We do not share, sell, or rent any user data to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy
Maven does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect personal data, it is safe for general use, but parental guidance is recommended for younger users.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within this document with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding these Terms or our Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our support channel or app listing page.
